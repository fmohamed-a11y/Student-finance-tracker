      About

      About pocketpetal, your student finance tracker

      Less money stress. More student life.

      pocketpetal is a one-page spending diary for students. Record purchases, see where your money goes, and get a nudge before you go over budget. Built with plain (vanilla) HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

      Nothing is sent to a server. Clearing site data removes your records, so your exported JSON backup is your way back.
